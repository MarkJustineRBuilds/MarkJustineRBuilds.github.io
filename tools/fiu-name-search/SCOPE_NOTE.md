# Scope note

- **Results relate only to the loaded client-list snapshot.** Each search uses the client list as it was at that moment. The version, load time and record count are recorded on every search and every PDF. People or entities missing from that list cannot be found.
- **A candidate is a possible name match, not an identity match.** Every candidate needs a human review that compares dates, nationality or country, identifiers and other information before any conclusion is drawn.
- **"No candidate found" is an engine result, not a human conclusion.** It means no record in the loaded list shared enough whole words with the name under the stated rules. It does not prove that the person or entity is unknown to your organisation.
- **The tool matches whole words only.** It does not do fuzzy, phonetic or transliteration matching. Spelling variants (MOHAMMED / MUHAMMAD) and English/Arabic equivalents are not matched. Consider these separately where your procedures require it.
- **It does not respond to the FIU.** Producing evidence is not a response. Any response, freeze or report is made by you, through your own procedures and the official channel.
